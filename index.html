Pose Off — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Summary: Pose Off does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app works offline and does not require an account.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising data.

Data stored on your device

Scenes, poses, presets, imported images, and captured photos are saved locally on your device in the app's private storage or in the location you choose. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party. You can delete this data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app may ask for storage access so you can import images or save photos to your device. Those files remain on your device.

Third-party services

The Full edition does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party SDKs that collect data. If you installed the separate Lite edition, that edition uses Google AdMob and its consent flow; this policy does not cover the Lite edition.

Children

Pose Off contains artistic 3D figure reference and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/30framesps/poseoff-privacy/issues.

Pose Off is a 3D posing and reference tool.
